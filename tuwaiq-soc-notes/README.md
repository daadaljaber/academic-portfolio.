# Tuwaiq Academy SOC Program — Personal Study Notes

**Daad Aljaber · 10 training days · 6–17 September 2026**

My English and Arabic study notes from the Tuwaiq Academy SOC program, organized by day. Topics progress from SOC fundamentals and networking to log analysis, SIEM, threat intelligence, incident triage and reporting.

## About this collection

These are personal learning notes, not an official Tuwaiq Academy publication, certification claim, or evidence of professional incident-response experience. Lab completion boxes reflect my Notion notes at export; unchecked activities remain unfinished. Practice queries and scenario templates are illustrative and have not been validated against a dataset in this repository.

The notes are shared as Markdown so they can be read directly on GitHub. Formatting has been adapted from Notion; workspace metadata and private Notion links are omitted. The Day 10 export omits one unavailable embedded item. No instructor slide decks or course attachments are republished here.
